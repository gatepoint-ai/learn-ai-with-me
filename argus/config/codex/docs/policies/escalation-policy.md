# Escalation Policy (ESC)

Fictional demo content for Argus. Who must be told about a revenue assurance finding, and how fast.

## ESC-1 Severity and response times

- **Critical** — escalate to the head of revenue assurance within one hour, and to the finance
  director the same day. Examples: a rating error on a whole customer class, a write-off above the
  approval limit with no approver recorded.
- **High** — the control owner investigates within one working day.
- **Medium** — reviewed in the next daily triage.
- **Low** — logged and reviewed weekly.

## ESC-2 Who owns what

The owner of the control that fired owns the finding until it is closed or handed over in writing.
Revenue assurance coordinates but does not own another team's process.

## ESC-3 Closing a finding

A finding is closed when its cause is explained and, if money was lost, the recovery action is
recorded. An alert that clears by itself because the data has changed is closed automatically, but
the owner still records whether the underlying problem was real.

## ESC-4 Reporting

Open critical and high findings are reported weekly to the head of revenue assurance, and monthly to
the finance committee with the revenue at risk and the amount recovered.
