# Write-off Policy (WO-POL)

Fictional demo content for Argus. This policy sets when uncollected revenue may be written off, who
may approve it, and what must be recorded.

## WO-POL-1 Principle

Revenue is written off only when collection has been genuinely attempted and has failed, or when
the cost of further collection exceeds the amount owed. A write-off is a decision, recorded with
its reason and approver. It is never a way to clear an ageing report.

## WO-POL-2 Approval limits

Approval depends on the total written off for one account in one month:

- Up to 500: the collections agent may approve, with the reason recorded on the account.
- 500 to 5,000: a credit control team lead must approve.
- Above 5,000: the head of credit control must approve, and the write-off is reported to the
  monthly finance committee.

Splitting one write-off into several smaller ones to stay under a limit is a breach of this policy.

## WO-POL-3 Re-rating and credits

When events were billed at the wrong rate, the difference is corrected by re-rating, not by writing
off. Re-rating more than 1,000 events, or any re-rating that reduces revenue by more than 10,000,
requires approval from billing operations and credit control together. The rating error that caused
it must be logged under the rating change procedure.

## WO-POL-4 Disputed revenue

Disputed revenue is not written off while the dispute is open. A dispute open for more than 60 days
is escalated to the team lead. If a dispute is resolved in the customer's favour, the amount is
credited, and the credit is recorded as a dispute outcome rather than a write-off, so the two can be
analysed separately.

## WO-POL-5 Records

Every write-off records the account, amount, period, reason code, approver and date. Reason codes
are: UNCOLLECTABLE, CUSTOMER_DECEASED, BANKRUPTCY, COST_EXCEEDS_DEBT and GOODWILL. A write-off with
no reason code is invalid and will be reversed by the monthly audit.
