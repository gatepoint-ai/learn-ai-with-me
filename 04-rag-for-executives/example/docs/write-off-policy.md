# Revenue write-off policy (fictional example)

## Approval limits

Write-offs up to 500 dollars may be approved by a team lead. Write-offs between 500 and 5,000 dollars require approval from the finance manager. Any write-off above 5,000 dollars must be approved by the chief financial officer.

## Required evidence

Every write-off request must include the original invoice number, the reason for the loss, and proof that at least two collection attempts were made.

## Reporting

Approved write-offs are reported to the finance committee at the end of each quarter.
